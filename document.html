Offerings
Configure services available to the customer

Offering: the combination of Asset, Service, and Waste. Offerings is the level that most customer and Service Area Interaction occurs. Offerings are what is assigned at the Service Area.

Offerings for Solid Waste, Recycle, and Yard Waste are assigned to a Service Area in the RSAUI and are available for selection in the CEUI.

When changes are made to Offerings in the RSAUI (such as adding or removing Assets), click the Refresh Assets button to retrieve the latest available updates. RSAUI Offering status changes will be updated in the CEUI.

For Service Categories not configured in the RSAUI, Offerings should only be added if the customer needs to call in to schedule service (e.g., Bulk, Christmas Tree).
